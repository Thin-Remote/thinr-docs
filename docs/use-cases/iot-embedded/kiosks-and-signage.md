# Kiosks and digital signage

Unattended displays in public spaces need reliable remote access: content updates, reboots, diagnostics, and incident response, all without sending a technician.

## What ThinRemote gives you

- Remote desktop access to diagnose frozen or misbehaving displays.
- Remote filesystem to push new assets or config files.
- Scheduled or on-demand reboots through `thinr device resource`.
- Fleet-level operations with products (update all devices of a signage line at once).
