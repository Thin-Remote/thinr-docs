# EV charging and smart meters

Chargers, inverters, and smart meters are high-availability assets deployed in public and private locations. Operators need to diagnose, reboot, and update without on-site intervention.

## What ThinRemote gives you

- Reliable outbound connectivity even on cellular uplinks.
- Remote logs, resource calls (reboot, reset session), and firmware updates.
- Alarm rules on connection loss, abnormal load, or temperature.
