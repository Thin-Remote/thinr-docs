# Vending and self-service machines

Vending and self-service machines need health monitoring (stock, payment status, errors) and occasional remote intervention without rolling a truck.

## What ThinRemote gives you

- Device-level health reporting through monitoring metrics and alarms.
- Remote terminal access for firmware troubleshooting.
- Fleet-wide operations: reboot or reconfigure all devices of a product at once.
