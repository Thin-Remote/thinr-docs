# Industrial PLCs and automation

Factories, water plants, and energy installations run controllers that are almost never directly reachable from the internet. They live behind NAT, industrial firewalls, or air-gapped segments with controlled uplinks.

## What ThinRemote gives you

- Outbound-only HTTPS connection: no IT ticket to open ports.
- SSH and HTTP tunneling to PLC web UIs from your own desk.
- Secure fleet monitoring of CPU, memory, and custom resources.
